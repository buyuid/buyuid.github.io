About Me

A developing research profile built around chemistry, education, scientific communication and practical innovation.

I am a Nigerian chemist, early-career researcher and chemistry educator. I completed my M.Sc. in Chemistry at Sokoto State University (2023–2025), following a B.Sc. in Pure Chemistry from Usmanu Danfodiyo University (2014–2017).

My postgraduate research focused on the isolation, characterization and antimicrobial study of Trigoniulus corallinus (red millipedes). My academic interests have also included natural coagulants for water treatment and water purification using Moringa oleifera.

Alongside research and teaching, I have participated in chemistry outreach, conferences, workshops and professional activities through organizations including the American Chemical Society and other scientific bodies.

“Developing scientifically sound, environmentally responsible and practically useful chemistry-based solutions.”

Featured M.Sc. Research

Isolation, Characterization and Antimicrobial Study of Trigoniulus corallinus (Red Millipedes)

The research investigated the chemical constituents and antimicrobial properties of extracts obtained from red millipedes, using extraction, screening, chromatographic separation and spectroscopic characterization.
