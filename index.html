About

Professional Profile

I am an Assistant Lecturer in the Department of Chemistry and Chemistry Education at Shehu Shagari University of Education, Sokoto, Nigeria. My academic interests combine chemistry research, science education, research communication and practical approaches to societal challenges.

My research experience includes natural products, phytochemical and zoochemical profiling, antimicrobial studies, sustainable chemistry and emerging applications of nanomaterials. I am particularly interested in research that can move from the laboratory toward useful, affordable and sustainable products.

I hold an M.Sc. in Chemistry from Sokoto State University, a B.Sc. in Pure Chemistry from Usmanu Danfodiyo University, and an NCE in Chemistry and Computer Science.
